YOUR OWN FORMATS · PATTERN

EMPLOYEE_ID

Anything a regular expression can describe, such as \bEMP-\d{6}\b.

What it knows out of the box

English, with spaCy's large model for names and places, and patterns and checksums for emails, phones and cards.
